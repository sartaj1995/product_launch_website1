PAGE STRUCTURE
1. Sticky header — logo mark + wordmark, section nav links, a primary "Notify Me"
   CTA button, and a hamburger menu that opens a full-screen mobile nav overlay.
2. Hero — eyebrow badge ("New · launch date"), large headline with a gradient

   form (client-side validation + success state, no backend needed), and the
   illustrated product visual with a "New" ribbon and a one-line disclaimer
   caption underneath it.
3. A horizontally auto-scrolling marquee strip of short claim chips (icon + text).
4. A 4-card feature/benefit grid (icon, title, one-line description each).
5. A two-column "story" section: a decorative abstract visual on one side, and on
   the other an eyebrow label, a tagline, a short paragraph, and a checklist of
   3 bullet points.
6. A certifications/trust grid (icon + label chips, generic/fictional).
7. An FAQ accordion (single-open-at-a-time, expand/collapse with icon rotation).
8. A closing full-width CTA section repeating the email capture form.
9. A footer — brand block with tagline and social icons, 1-2 link columns, and a
   bottom bar with copyright plus a short disclaimer noting this is a fictional
   demo/portfolio project.

- A condensed/bold display font for headings paired with a regular sans for body

- Scroll-reveal animations on section entry, respecting prefers-reduced-motion.
- Fully responsive (mobile/tablet/desktop), accessible (focus states, alt/aria
